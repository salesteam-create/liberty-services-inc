Overview

Clean filters without the cleanup.

Filters need to be cleaned on a regular schedule and cleaned down to bare metal. Many kitchens do not have the time or staff to keep up, and when filters are cleaned on the premises, the resulting grease ends up down the drain.

With filter exchange, your filters are cleaned off site. That lowers the amount of grease going down your drain by up to 60-70%, saves your team hours every week and keeps airflow where it should be.

FAQs

Filter Exchange questions, answered.

How often are filters exchanged?
Most kitchens swap filters every one to four weeks depending on cooking volume. We set a schedule during your survey and adjust it as your volume changes.
Do I own the filters?
Exchange programs use a rotating set of professionally cleaned stainless steel filters, so you never have to buy, store or replace your own.
Why not just wash filters in-house?
Washing filters on site sends grease down your drains, takes staff time and rarely gets filters to bare metal. Off-site cleaning keeps up to 60-70% of that grease out of your plumbing.
