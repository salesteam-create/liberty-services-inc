NFPA 96 compliance

How often should your kitchen hood be cleaned?

NFPA 96 is the fire code standard for commercial kitchen exhaust. It sets a minimum cleaning frequency based on what and how much you cook. Your local fire marshal, landlord or insurer may require more.

Liberty Services cleans every system to meet or exceed NFPA 96, and we set your schedule during a free site survey so you are never guessing.

FAQs

Hood Cleaning questions, answered.

How long does a hood cleaning take?
Most single-hood systems take three to five hours. Larger kitchens with multiple hoods or long duct runs can take a full overnight shift. We confirm timing during your site survey.
How often should a commercial kitchen hood be cleaned?
NFPA 96 sets the minimum: monthly for solid-fuel cooking, quarterly for high-volume operations (24-hour, charbroiling, wok), semi-annually for moderate volume and annually for low-volume kitchens. Your local fire marshal or insurer may require more.
Do I need to prepare the kitchen?
Just turn off cooking equipment and let it cool before we arrive. We cover equipment, protect floors and handle the rest, including cleanup.
Will you put a sticker on the hood?
Yes. After every cleaning we apply a dated service label to the hood, and your full photo report and certificate are uploaded to your customer portal.
