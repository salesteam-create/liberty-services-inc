Overview

Once grease reaches your roof, it costs you.

Uncontained grease can cause extensive damage to your roof, make it deteriorate faster and pose a fire hazard. When it rains or snow melts, that grease runs off into the sewer system and can put you in violation of EPA codes.

Liberty Services certified technicians assess where your exhaust system meets your roof and install a grease containment system that collects and contains grease while allowing rainwater to drain and run off.

FAQs

Grease Management questions, answered.

Why is grease on my roof a problem?
Rooftop grease breaks down roofing materials, creates a fire hazard and washes into storm drains when it rains or snow melts, which can put you in violation of EPA codes.
What kind of containment do I need?
It depends on your fan type, roof material and cooking volume. Our technicians assess where your exhaust meets the roof and recommend the right mix of interceptors, grease boxes and gutters.
Do you service containment after it is installed?
Yes. We can replace filter media and clean containment units on the same visit as your hood cleaning, so you only schedule once.
