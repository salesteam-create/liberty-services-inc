Overview

When the fan stops, the kitchen stops.

A worn belt or failing motor can shut down your exhaust and your cooking line with it. Grease-loaded fans also run out of balance, which wears out bearings and shortens equipment life.

Our technicians inspect your rooftop equipment every time we clean, flag issues before they become failures and handle repairs on the spot when possible. We also install the hinge kits and access panels NFPA 96 calls for, so every future cleaning is safe and complete.

FAQs

Rooftop Services questions, answered.

Why does my exhaust fan need hinge kits?
NFPA 96 requires safe access to clean the fan. A hinge kit lets technicians tilt the fan open without damaging it or the curb, and it makes every future cleaning faster and safer.
Can you repair a fan that stopped working?
Yes. We diagnose and replace belts, motors and bearings, and we will tell you when a repair no longer makes sense compared with a replacement.
Do you clean up the roof after service?
Always. We remove grease, debris and used materials and leave the area around your exhaust fan clean and protected.
