NFPA 96 compliance

We meet or exceed NFPA 96 standards.

NFPA 96 is the fire code standard for commercial kitchen exhaust. It sets a minimum cleaning frequency based on what and how much you cook. Your local fire marshal, landlord or insurer may require more.

Liberty Services cleans every system to meet or exceed NFPA 96, and we set your schedule during a free site survey so you are never guessing.
