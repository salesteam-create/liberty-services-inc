NFPA 96 cleaning frequency: how often should your hood be cleaned?

Monthly, quarterly, semi-annually or annually? Here is how NFPA 96 sets your schedule and what pushes a kitchen into a higher tier.

By the Liberty Services teamOctober 2, 20266 min read

If you run a commercial kitchen, NFPA 96 is the standard your fire marshal and insurer use to judge whether your exhaust system is safe. One of the first questions it answers is how often that system needs to be inspected and cleaned.

The four NFPA 96 frequency tiers

  • Monthly: systems serving solid-fuel cooking such as wood-fired ovens, charcoal grills and pizza ovens.
  • Quarterly: high-volume operations including 24-hour kitchens, charbroiling and wok cooking.
  • Semi-annually: moderate-volume cooking, which covers most full-service restaurants.
  • Annually: low-volume kitchens such as churches, day camps and seasonal operations.

What moves you into a higher tier

Volume is not just about hours. Menu matters. A kitchen that adds charbroiled items or extends late-night service can produce far more grease vapor than it did a year ago. If your inspection photos show heavy buildup before your next scheduled visit, your frequency is too low.

The right schedule is the one that keeps grease from building up between visits, not the one that is easiest to remember.

Your local rules can be stricter

NFPA 96 sets the minimum. Local fire codes, landlords and insurance carriers can and often do require more frequent service. Always check your lease and policy, and keep your service records where you can find them quickly.
